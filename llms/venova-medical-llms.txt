# Venova Medical

> Venova Medical, Inc. is a privately held, clinical-stage medical device company in Los Gatos, California, developing the Velocity Percutaneous AVF System — a minimally invasive device for creating an arteriovenous fistula for hemodialysis vascular access. Venova is a physical device manufacturer and publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK or machine-readable specification.
